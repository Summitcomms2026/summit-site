## S05-P021 — Conversion Without the Clutter
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_05_Applied_AI_Investment_Maison_Due_Diligence_v01.docx table 21 -->

### Website copy

PUBLIC-FACING COPY
ENQUIRIES
Conversion Without the Clutter
Near the Top
Primary enquiry plus one relevant secondary route. Both visible without an excessive button row.
Within the Page
One optional interactive guide. A tailored CTA appears when the visitor has chosen a concern.
Lower on the Page
Three related routes then one specific enquiry invitation. Maintain context from the page and chosen interaction.
For Editorial Pages
Newsletter subscription and LinkedIn sharing are appropriate on research or essays; no subscription diversion inside acquisition enquiries.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

CLIFF NOTES / PRODUCTION
LEAD CAPTURE  Single short enquiry form, preselected subject, editable summary, explicit submit. Clear success only after a working backend confirms it.
MEASUREMENT  Consent-aware events: view, tool completion, related link, enquiry open, enquiry submitted. Do not conflate opens with submissions.
ACCESSIBILITY  Keyboard-friendly interaction, visible focus, labels, small-screen stacking and screen-reader feedback for changed results.
CLAIMS  No AI adoption estimates, proprietary score output or investment-return promises.

Gated: complete approved PDF where the publisher has authorised distribution.

SEO  Index public summaries and findings. Do not conceal the only substantive text behind a modal.
PRIVACY  Store the minimum data; publication consent and communications consent must be separately recorded.

## S09-P031 — Every essay is a permanent editorial page with a canonical address, metadata and its own reading journey.
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_09_Future_of_Luxury_Copy_FAQs_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 31 -->

### Website copy

What to build
Every essay is a permanent editorial page with a canonical address, metadata and its own reading journey.
Every subject has a reliable entry point through search or a curated topic page.
Every article offers related reading, a discreet newsletter invitation and — only where relevant — a link to Summit’s work.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

CLIFF NOTES
Build requirements: server-rendered article pages and paginated archive, crawlable HTML links, sitemap, canonical URLs, Article structured data, accessible filters, keyboard search and no-index policies for faceted combinations.
Search can use full text in production; metadata-only matching is sufficient for the prototype.
Important: do not ship the prototype’s mailto subscription as the production newsletter integration.

## S09-P032 — The useful question is not how many people reached an article, but what they did next.
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_09_Future_of_Luxury_Copy_FAQs_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 32 -->

### Website copy

Conversion measurement
The useful question is not how many people reached an article, but what they did next.
Internal measures: article reading completion, related-article journeys, filter/search use, newsletter confirmations, meaningful enquiry starts and qualified conversations.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

CLIFF NOTES
Use aggregated, consent-respecting analytics. Avoid deriving visitor identity or intent from private-reading behaviour without proper notice.
Cross-sell only contextually: Hermès ownership → Maison Due Diligence; Chanel/Charvet → Investment & Acquisitions; Porsche → Reposition a Maison; Louis Vuitton → Activate Patrimony & IP.
Enquiry remains person-first: “What are you considering?” before “What is your budget?”
