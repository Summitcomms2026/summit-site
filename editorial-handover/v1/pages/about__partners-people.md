## S10-P028 — Who leads an engagement?
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_10_About_Copy_FAQs_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 28 -->

### Website copy

PUBLIC WEBSITE COPY   /   FAQ ACCORDIONS
Who leads an engagement?
Summit agrees the lead responsibility and team structure with the client at the outset. The people assigned should match the judgement and specialist work the brief requires; roles should not be inferred from a website directory.
Are all named collaborators Summit employees?
No. Some specialists operate independent practices and are engaged where appropriate. Their professional status and responsibilities should be stated clearly. The client-facing relationship and scope are agreed before work begins.
Who handles intellectual-property advice?
Luxury IP and patrimony work may involve independent specialist collaborators such as Olivia Dhordain of Outboxing-IP. Formal legal opinions and regulated advice require the appropriately qualified professional and engagement structure; Summit’s research is not a substitute.
Can the team work across jurisdictions?
Summit’s work concerns internationally operating clients, with appropriate collaborators and specialists brought in where relevant. Project jurisdiction, delivery arrangements and any legal or regulatory requirements are discussed during scoping, rather than assumed.
