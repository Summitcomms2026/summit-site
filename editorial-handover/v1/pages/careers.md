## S11-P015 — Are you hiring?
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_11_Lets_Talk_Utility_Footer_Copy_FAQs_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 15 -->

### Website copy

PUBLIC WEBSITE COPY   /   FAQ ACCORDIONS
Are you hiring?
Any current opportunities should appear here once they are approved for publication. If no roles are listed, that should not be read as a hidden vacancy.
Do you work with independent specialists?
Summit works with specialist expertise when a brief calls for it. Expressions of interest are welcome, but should not be taken as evidence of an active commission or a standing place on the team.
Can I send a portfolio rather than a CV?
A concise summary of relevant work and a portfolio link may be more useful for some creative and specialist disciplines. A particular advertised position may have its own application requirements.
How will application details be used?
Applicant information must be handled under a separately approved recruitment privacy notice. Its storage period, access and deletion arrangements should be stated before the site begins collecting applications.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

CLIFF NOTES   /   FAQ BEHAVIOUR
LAYOUT  Native, accessible accordion rows; answers remain indexable and readable without submitting a form.
INTERACTION  Keyboard support, clear focus, correct expanded state and restrained chevron. Do not use a button merely to decorate.
MOBILE  Full-width tap targets, short question lines and comfortable text measure.
EDIT  Do not use FAQs to make policy or contractual claims before operations and legal approval.
CONVERSION  A quiet contextual CTA may follow the complete FAQ section; never interrupt an expanded answer.
