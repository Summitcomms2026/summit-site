## S11-P010 — Who can use Client Access?
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_11_Lets_Talk_Utility_Footer_Copy_FAQs_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 10 -->

### Website copy

PUBLIC WEBSITE COPY   /   FAQ ACCORDIONS
Who can use Client Access?
Existing clients who have been invited to the approved client environment. Access depends on the permissions established for the particular engagement.
Can I create an account myself?
Account creation should follow an invitation or an approved onboarding process. The public website should not offer self-registration until that workflow has been verified.
What if I cannot sign in?
Use the approved support route or contact your Summit representative. Do not send passwords, verification codes or confidential documents through the public contact form.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

CLIFF NOTES   /   FAQ BEHAVIOUR
LAYOUT  Native, accessible accordion rows; answers remain indexable and readable without submitting a form.
INTERACTION  Keyboard support, clear focus, correct expanded state and restrained chevron. Do not use a button merely to decorate.
MOBILE  Full-width tap targets, short question lines and comfortable text measure.
EDIT  Do not use FAQs to make policy or contractual claims before operations and legal approval.
CONVERSION  A quiet contextual CTA may follow the complete FAQ section; never interrupt an expanded answer.
