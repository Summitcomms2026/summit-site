## S01-P001 — Build What Deserves to Last

### Website copy

SUMMIT COMMUNICATION GROUP
Build What Deserves to Last
Summit Communication Group advises the people who build, lead and own luxury businesses. Our work spans brand, growth, experience and patrimony — the decisions that shape what a maison becomes.
Explore Solutions  →   /   Discuss a Brief  →

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

ROLE  Position Summit in a single reading.
IMAGE  One arresting, original image: a material, architectural or making detail with its own sense of permanence. Avoid handbags, watches or cars presented as borrowed prestige.
LAYOUT  Full-width hero. Large, quiet serif headline; short body in a narrow measure. Copy on an intentionally clear area of the image or on an adjacent solid field.
ACTIONS  Primary: Explore Solutions. Secondary: Discuss a Brief. Equal access, unequal emphasis.
ICONS  None. A restrained arrow after each link is sufficient.
MOBILE  Image first only if the opening sentence remains visible. Otherwise stack headline, text, image, actions.
GUARDRAIL  Do not imply named client relationships by using a recognisable product image.

### Website copy

SOLUTIONS
What’s the Brief?
Every brief starts somewhere: an ambition, a problem or a decision that cannot wait. Find the work by need, function or industry.
By Need  The decision before you.  → Explore Needs
By Function  The expertise required.  → Explore Functions
By Industry  The world in which you operate.  → Explore Industries

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

ROLE  Help a visitor find the relevant engagement without knowing agency terminology.
IMAGE  None necessary. This is a navigational panel, not a gallery.
LAYOUT  Three equal columns on desktop. Lead with By Need visually and in keyboard order; functions and industries remain directly accessible.
ACTIONS  Each column is a distinct route to its own index. No generic “Discover more” button.
ICONS  Use a modest directional arrow; avoid sector pictograms or decorative diamonds.
MOBILE  Three stacked cards or a clearly labelled accordion. Keep all routes visible.
GUARDRAIL  Retain all 10 Need entries, 6 Function entries and 8 Industry entries in the underlying sitemap; the homepage is only a route.

## S01-P004 — What Valuation Misses

### Website copy

THE OBSERVATORY
What Valuation Misses
Price can be measured. Authority is harder. The Observatory examines the objects, institutions and decisions through which cultural meaning is earned, carried or diminished.
Explore The Observatory  →   /   Request a Private Briefing  →

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

ROLE  Show why Summit thinks differently and introduce a high-trust conversion.
IMAGE  Original visual from the research world: archival sheets, reading notes, typographic data or a considered photograph of the books. Avoid dashboard screenshots and decorative AI imagery.
LAYOUT  Contrasting full-width feature; 50/50 type and visual or an asymmetric 60/40 arrangement. Visibly different from the Solutions navigation.
ACTIONS  Editorial route plus a more private conversion. “Request a Private Briefing” should open a dedicated Observatory enquiry.
ICONS  None; use a fine rule to mark the section.
MOBILE  Body, then two vertically stacked links; the research visual may move below the text.
GUARDRAIL  Do not imply that Observatory readings predict investment returns or that aura can be manufactured. Validate any live numerical counter before display.

IMAGE  Type-led. If any imagery is used, one restrained overarching photograph rather than six unrelated thumbnails.

Research to Challenge
Touch Me Not asks when an object becomes more than its materials. The Luxury 100 tests that question against a declared method. The Observatory examines the wider field. Each is intended to be read, examined and challenged.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

ROLE  Distinguish proprietary scholarship from routine agency thought leadership.
IMAGE  Use the actual book jackets, original illustrated pages and designed covers. Credit Olivia Dhordain for her artwork where used.

## S01-P007 — Beyond the Headlines
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_01_Homepage_Copy_and_Cliff_Notes_v03.docx table 7 -->

### Website copy

THE FUTURE OF LUXURY
Beyond the Headlines
Essays on the decisions, contradictions and quiet changes shaping luxury — where culture, capital, creativity and institutional memory meet.
—  Chanel Bought Charvet Because Hermès Was Right All Along
—  Porsche Called 911. Ferrari Never Forgot It Was Luxury
—  Louis Vuitton Won the Lawsuit. China Decided It Was Wrong
Read the Essays  →   /   Subscribe  →

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

ROLE  Invite regular reading and provide contemporary proof of judgement.
IMAGE  One distinct, rights-cleared editorial image per essay: cloth/construction, automotive form and historic ornament. Avoid unlicensed campaign photographs.
LAYOUT  Three-column magazine-style article strip, preferably with one featured story stronger than the other two. Use text-first cards if rights are pending.
ACTIONS  Each headline opens its essay. Read the Essays and Subscribe are separate, plain-text invitations.
ICONS  Tiny directional arrows only. No social-sharing clutter on the homepage.
MOBILE  One featured article followed by two compact teasers. All three remain accessible without horizontal scrolling.
GUARDRAIL  Article headlines must be exact and stories fact-checked again before launch if developments have moved on.

### Website copy

LET’S TALK
What’s at Stake for You?
An emerging maison, an established icon, an acquisition under consideration or a business facing change. We begin with a considered conversation about the matter at hand.
Discuss a Brief  →   /   Private Enquiries  →

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

ROLE  Offer a private way in, without an inflated sales promise.
IMAGE  Prefer no photograph. Quiet space and typography are the appropriate finish.
LAYOUT  Spacious full-width closing panel. Two adjacent links or one primary button and one unobtrusive email/contact route.
ACTIONS  Primary Discuss a Brief → Let’s Talk. Secondary Private Enquiries → discreet contact pathway, not a public scheduling widget.
ICONS  None.
MOBILE  Single narrow copy measure; primary link full tap width.
GUARDRAIL  Promise confidentiality only to the extent supported by the actual enquiry process and privacy handling.
