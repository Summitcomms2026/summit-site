Two Different Readings
The Luxury 100
A Canon of objects selected for their enduring cultural and institutional significance. It asks what belongs at the centre, under a declared method.
The Observatory
A wider field examined through the same evidential discipline. It asks what surrounds that centre and how different sectors behave.
The distinction matters. One is an argument about the centre. The other is a continuing inquiry into the territory around it.

## S06-P004 — How is The Observatory different from The Luxury 100?
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_06_Observatory_Copy_FAQs_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 4 -->

### Website copy

PUBLIC-FACING COPY
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Questions Worth Asking
How is The Observatory different from The Luxury 100?
The Luxury 100 is a defended Canon: a ranked argument about a defined group of objects. The Observatory examines the wider field around that Canon, sector by sector. Its purpose is not to supply another winners’ list but to reveal patterns of governance, origination, scarcity, mythology and time.
Is this another luxury brand valuation?
No. A valuation estimates economic value for a stated purpose. The Observatory examines the institutional conditions that may help an object or maison retain meaning over time. Those readings can inform a broader commercial conversation, but they neither replace a valuation nor produce an investment return forecast.

## S06-P005 — Can the research tell us why an icon has weakened?
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_06_Observatory_Copy_FAQs_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 5 -->

### Website copy

PUBLIC-FACING COPY
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Questions Worth Asking
Can the research tell us why an icon has weakened?
It can help frame that question. The method separates an object’s own standing from the prestige of the maison carrying it, then looks at decisions around distribution, narrative control, patrimony and governance. An explanation still needs evidence; a famous name is not evidence by itself.
Can we use the research privately?
Potentially. A private briefing begins with the decision you are weighing and the evidence available. We can discuss whether a focused institutional reading would be useful, what it might cover and where other professional advisers would be required.
