## S06-P016 — What May Not Appear in the Accounts
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_06_Observatory_Copy_FAQs_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 16 -->

### Website copy

PUBLIC-FACING COPY
FOR FAMILY OFFICES & INVESTORS
What May Not Appear in the Accounts
Financial statements answer important questions. They do not always show the whole story of a maison’s reputation, inherited knowledge or fragility.
Under IAS 38, internally generated brands are generally not recognised as intangible assets. That is an accounting boundary, not proof of a hidden fortune. It does, however, make the quality of the institution worth examining in its own right.
Continuity
Will the craft, judgement and relationships survive a change of ownership?
Control
What can be protected — and what exists only through custom, confidentiality or continuing practice?
Carriage
Does the object sustain the house, or is the brand paying to keep the object culturally alive?

## S06-P018 — Is this a form of investment due diligence?
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_06_Observatory_Copy_FAQs_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 18 -->

### Website copy

PUBLIC-FACING COPY
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Questions Worth Asking
Is this a form of investment due diligence?
It is a complementary institutional reading, not a substitute for financial, legal, tax, technical or valuation diligence. The Observatory can help identify what a maison’s cultural and commercial standing rests on, where evidence is thin and which decisions may affect its continuity.
Can you calculate what a maison is worth?
No. The method reads institutional standing, not enterprise value. Price, return and capital structure depend on additional information and professional valuation methods. We do not turn a canonical reading into a forecast of purchase price or investment performance.

## S06-P019 — How do you assess risk when evidence is incomplete?
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_06_Observatory_Copy_FAQs_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 19 -->

### Website copy

PUBLIC-FACING COPY
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Questions Worth Asking
How do you assess risk when evidence is incomplete?
By making the gap explicit. The Observatory’s research distinguishes supported readings from provisional or unresolved cases, rather than assigning certainty that the evidence cannot sustain. A private engagement should do the same: state what is known, what remains unverified and what further examination may require.
Can a family office approach you before a transaction is public?
You can raise the question in general terms without identifying a counterparty or sharing confidential material. If further work is appropriate, scope, confidentiality and any secure information exchange should be agreed separately before sensitive documents are provided.
