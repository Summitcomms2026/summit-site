## S06-P039 — Do I need a detailed brief?
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_06_Observatory_Copy_FAQs_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 39 -->

### Website copy

PUBLIC-FACING COPY
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Questions Worth Asking
Do I need a detailed brief?
No. A decision, concern or question is enough. It is often more useful to begin with what is unclear than to send a lengthy document that already assumes the answer.
Is the first conversation a sales presentation?
It should not be. We begin by listening and testing our understanding of the question. Sometimes the right next step is a defined piece of work. Sometimes it is simply a clearer way of looking at the issue.

## S06-P040 — Should I send confidential information now?
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_06_Observatory_Copy_FAQs_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 40 -->

### Website copy

PUBLIC-FACING COPY
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Questions Worth Asking
Should I send confidential information now?
Not through the public form. An outline of the subject is sufficient. If the discussion calls for sensitive records, legal advice or deal materials, appropriate arrangements should be agreed before those are exchanged.
Will I be expected to appoint Summit?
No. The purpose of the initial discussion is to discover whether there is a useful fit. A worthwhile conversation should improve clarity even if neither side proposes an engagement.
