## S06-P025 — Does The Field rank luxury brands?
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_06_Observatory_Copy_FAQs_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 25 -->

### Website copy

PUBLIC-FACING COPY
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Questions Worth Asking
Does The Field rank luxury brands?
No. Its primary unit is the sector and its governed object propositions, not a leaderboard of companies. It examines the distribution of institutional conditions within and across sectors. Canonical rank belongs to The Luxury 100, a separate instrument with its own admissions and published method.
Why do some entries remain unresolved?
Because the relevant evidence cannot responsibly be inferred. A dated product form, workshop record or primary-source confirmation may be missing. The field records those limits instead of completing the picture with assumptions. Unresolved status is a methodological distinction, not a judgement on the quality of the house.

## S06-P026 — Is the Outer Field part of the scored Observatory?
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_06_Observatory_Copy_FAQs_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 26 -->

### Website copy

PUBLIC-FACING COPY
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Questions Worth Asking
Is the Outer Field part of the scored Observatory?
No. The governed OBS Field is scored under its method. The Outer Field, referred to as the 1000, records reserve, adjacent and provisional candidates without scoring them. The two are separately governed and should not be combined into one ranking.
Can we ask about a particular sector?
Yes. A useful briefing might examine the institutional conditions in one category or test a question about a proposed maison against relevant sector patterns. The answer depends on the question, available evidence and the boundaries of the field as it stands.
