## S11-P024 — Will a contact enquiry add me to the newsletter?
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_11_Lets_Talk_Utility_Footer_Copy_FAQs_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 24 -->

### Website copy

PUBLIC WEBSITE COPY   /   FAQ ACCORDIONS
Will a contact enquiry add me to the newsletter?
It should not. A newsletter subscription is a separate, optional action and should be recorded and managed independently of the reason you contacted Summit.
Do I need to accept analytics cookies to contact Summit?
A visitor should not have to accept optional analytics or advertising technology to send an enquiry. The final implementation should be tested against the actual cookie and consent configuration.
Can I change a newsletter preference later?
The newsletter service should provide a clear unsubscribe route and any applicable preference controls. Those controls must be connected and tested before a subscription form goes live.
Where is my information stored?
The published policy must identify the actual systems, service providers and relevant cross-border arrangements. Those details cannot be supplied accurately until the website’s production stack and service contracts are confirmed.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

CLIFF NOTES   /   FAQ BEHAVIOUR
LAYOUT  Native, accessible accordion rows; answers remain indexable and readable without submitting a form.
INTERACTION  Keyboard support, clear focus, correct expanded state and restrained chevron. Do not use a button merely to decorate.
MOBILE  Full-width tap targets, short question lines and comfortable text measure.
EDIT  Do not use FAQs to make policy or contractual claims before operations and legal approval.
CONVERSION  A quiet contextual CTA may follow the complete FAQ section; never interrupt an expanded answer.
