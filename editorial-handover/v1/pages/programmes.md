PROGRAMMES
Work With a Defined Purpose
Some decisions call for a fresh view. Others need several disciplines working together over time.
Our programmes give that work a clear starting point. Each is shaped around a specific institutional or commercial need, with the right people involved and an agreed scope of work.
Begin with what needs attention. We can decide together whether a programme is the appropriate answer.
Find Your Starting Point  →
Discuss a Programme  →

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

CLIFF NOTES / PRODUCTION
IMAGE  Real archive or studio work in progress. An editorial view of people engaged in the work; avoid anonymous boardrooms and generic luxury goods.
LAYOUT  Full-width opening copy with controlled measure and one strong image; no carousel before the programme choices.
INTERACTION  “Find Your Starting Point” opens the simple programme selector prototype. Not a diagnostic, quotation engine or qualification gate.
ENQUIRY  Secondary CTA leads directly to Let’s Talk, with programme undecided allowed.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

CLIFF NOTES / PRODUCTION
LAYOUT  Three-column plain-language process. No pipeline graphic, numbered “method” or gratuitous icons.
CONVERSION  Place before the final set of programme cards or as a slim transitional panel.
PROMISE  Do not imply free research, written assessment, legal opinion or a workshop at first contact.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

CLIFF NOTES / PRODUCTION
FORM  /lets-talk?programme=undecided. Preselect programme and allow the visitor to change it. No budget requirement.
TONE  Listen first. Aim to clarify what deserves attention whether or not Summit is ultimately engaged.
PRIVACY  Seek a short non-confidential description only; confirm any sensitive material through separate secure arrangements.
