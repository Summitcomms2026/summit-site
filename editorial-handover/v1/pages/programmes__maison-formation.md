## S07-P010 — Is this intended only for new luxury brands?
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_07_Programmes_Copy_FAQs_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 10 -->

### Website copy

PUBLIC-FACING COPY
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Good Questions
Is this intended only for new luxury brands?
It is most useful where a business has a serious luxury proposition but has not yet established the commercial and institutional arrangements needed to sustain it. That can include a founder-led venture, a family enterprise or an existing brand entering a more demanding stage.
Do we need a finished product before we begin?
Not necessarily. Part of the work is deciding whether the proposed object, signature or service can carry the identity being claimed. A promising concept may need further product discipline before communication or scale makes sense.

## S07-P011 — How is this different from a rebrand?
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_07_Programmes_Copy_FAQs_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 11 -->

### Website copy

PUBLIC-FACING COPY
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Good Questions
How is this different from a rebrand?
A rebrand may change identity, language or presentation. Maison Formation looks further upstream and downstream: what is being made, who will want it, how it is sold, what can be protected and how the institution will remain coherent once the founder is no longer making every decision.
What do we receive at the end?
The source practice sets out a diagnostic with a cultural-source reading, product assessment, revenue-quality assessment, experience review, IP and patrimony exposure register, governance priorities and staged action plan. The exact outputs are agreed against the brief, with specialist legal work scoped separately where required.
