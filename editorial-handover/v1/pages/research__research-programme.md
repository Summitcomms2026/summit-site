## S08-P027 — How the Work Is Governed

### Website copy

PUBLIC COPY  /  RESEARCH PROGRAMME
How the Work Is Governed
Define the Question  Name the unit of analysis, the relevant evidence and what would count against the initial interpretation.
Read the Evidence  Distinguish public sources, original observation and material supplied by participants.
Record the Limits  State uncertainty, open cases, exceptions and where a conclusion would overreach.
Publish Responsibly  Separate public findings from client-specific advice. A commissioned engagement cannot purchase a public research conclusion.

## S08-P029 — Can my maison propose a research question?
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_08_Research_Copy_FAQs_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 29 -->

### Website copy

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Good Questions
Can my maison propose a research question?
Yes. We can consider a question, explain what evidence it might require and whether it belongs in public research or a private engagement. Suggesting a topic does not guarantee a study.
Will confidential material appear in a publication?
Not without appropriate permission and an agreed process. The initial website enquiry should contain no confidential documents or sensitive commercial details.
Can clients influence the public Canon?
No. Paid advisory work cannot determine admissions, ranks or published Observatory judgements. That separation is essential to the research’s credibility.
When will new papers be published?
Only when the evidence and editorial review justify publication. Each confirmed paper should carry a real status and date; we will not invent a calendar to give the appearance of momentum.
