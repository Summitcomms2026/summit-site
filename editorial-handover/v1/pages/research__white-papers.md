## S08-P022 — Are the papers free to read?
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_08_Research_Copy_FAQs_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 22 -->

### Website copy

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Good Questions
Are the papers free to read?
The website should make each paper’s access terms clear. The proposed default is complimentary access after a short request form; some research may instead be published openly.
Will I be subscribed to marketing if I request a paper?
No. Receiving a paper and subscribing to further updates are separate choices. Newsletter consent should be optional and unchecked.
Can I share the paper with colleagues?
The permitted uses appear with each publication. Share the public summary freely; distribution of the complete PDF remains subject to the rights stated in that edition.
Can Summit prepare research on a question specific to us?
Possibly. A conversation can establish the decision, available evidence, confidentiality needs and whether a commissioned research engagement is appropriate.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

CLIFF NOTES  /  PRODUCTION
LEGAL  Publication-specific rights notice required. Do not assert universal reproduction permission.
FAQ  Accordions beneath the open preview and outside the form; no gating of basic questions.
