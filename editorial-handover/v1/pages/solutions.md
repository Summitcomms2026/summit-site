## S02-P001 — What Needs to Change?
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_02_Solutions_By_Need_Build_a_Maison_v02.docx table 1 -->

### Website copy

SOLUTIONS
What Needs to Change?
Building a maison, restoring its position or planning its next chapter: each raises different questions. Some involve brand strategy; others concern revenue, experience, IP or governance. Start with the decision in front of you. The disciplines can follow.
By Need
The challenge the business is facing.
By Function
The expertise the work requires.
By Industry
The sector in which the question arises.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

PURPOSE  Lead with the business question. Do not make a visitor diagnose which department to hire.
IMAGE  No image required. The routes need space and instant clarity.
LAYOUT  Header plus three navigation columns. “By Need” is the default selected route; “By Function” and “By Industry” remain equally discoverable.
CTA  Each route opens its corresponding index. Label the destination exactly as shown.
ICONS  No sector pictograms. A discreet directional arrow for linked headings is enough.
MOBILE  Three clear links, stacked. All options visible without interaction.
CHECK  Do not describe the ten Needs as programmes: they are client questions which may lead to several disciplines.
