## S13-P065 — Beyond Visibility
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_13_Industry_Function_Copy_and_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 65 -->

### Website copy

BEYOND VISIBILITY
Beyond Visibility
Positioning, campaign language, creative codes, distribution and product narrative must agree. When they do not, greater reach makes the inconsistency easier to notice. We examine what the brand has permission to say, whom it needs to persuade and whether the offer supports the message. For established maisons, the task may be less about launching another campaign and more about controlling which associations and channels deserve access to the brand.

## S13-P069 — Questions We Hear
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_13_Industry_Function_Copy_and_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 69 -->

### Website copy

FAQs
Questions We Hear
How do we protect exclusivity while increasing sales?
By choosing where demand should come from and how the experience supports price and identity. Better commercial discipline is not the same as withholding all growth.
Is branding just visual identity?
No. Identity is one expression of positioning. Product, distribution, claims and client treatment are also part of what the brand means.
How should success be assessed?
Against the agreed objective, which may include qualified demand, price integrity, client relationships or clarity of positioning rather than impressions alone.
