## S13-P086 — Discovery to Relationship

### Website copy

DISCOVERY TO RELATIONSHIP
Discovery to Relationship
Search, product pages, appointment booking, checkout, CRM and aftercare are rarely planned as one coherent experience. Yet a client notices every break in the sequence. We examine where relevant demand comes from, how products are explained, what makes the transaction trustworthy and which interactions should remain personal. Growth tactics need to be judged by their effect on price, privacy and client quality as well as their near-term conversion figures.

## S13-P090 — Questions We Hear
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_13_Industry_Function_Copy_and_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 90 -->

### Website copy

FAQs
Questions We Hear
Is conversion optimisation at odds with luxury?
No. Removing confusion or unnecessary friction strengthens service. The risk lies in tactics that win a transaction by weakening the longer-term proposition.
Should every purchase be fully self-service?
Not necessarily. Some clients value human assistance or an appointment. The best pathway gives appropriate choice without awkward handoffs.
Can we start without replacing the platform?
Often. A review can identify content, journey or data problems that should be resolved before deciding whether technical change is justified.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

ACCORDION
Three manually edited questions. Closed by default, keyboard-operable and individually expandable. Keep questions indexable in the delivered HTML, not hidden behind client-side-only data.
No FAQ schema unless current search-engine eligibility and policy have been verified by the build team.
