## S13-P072 — From Opinion to Authority
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_13_Industry_Function_Copy_and_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 72 -->

### Website copy

FROM OPINION TO AUTHORITY
From Opinion to Authority
A serious editorial programme needs a recognisable voice, reliable sources and a reason to exist beyond keeping a channel active. We consider who should speak, which claims can be supported, how an argument develops across formats and when silence is more credible than another comment on the news. Executive voices in luxury and finance need particular discipline: an article that earns attention but cannot withstand a basic fact-check is a poor investment in authority.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

PURPOSE
Describe a real sector or discipline problem before presenting Summit’s services. Keep this panel largely typographic; do not insert decorative icons.

## S13-P076 — Questions We Hear
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_13_Industry_Function_Copy_and_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 76 -->

### Website copy

FAQs
Questions We Hear
Is thought leadership the same as PR?
No. Public relations can help an argument reach the right audience. Thought leadership depends first on the quality, originality and evidence of the argument.
Can we write for an executive who has little time?
Yes, where the ideas are genuinely theirs and a sound interview and review process is possible. We do not manufacture expertise or attribute unsupported opinions.
Should every article have a commercial CTA?
Not every article needs a sales invitation. Useful related reading, a considered subscription or a relevant conversation may be a better next step.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

Three manually edited questions. Closed by default, keyboard-operable and individually expandable. Keep questions indexable in the delivered HTML, not hidden behind client-side-only data.
