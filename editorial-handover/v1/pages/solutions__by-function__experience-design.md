## S13-P079 — The Gaps Clients Remember
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_13_Industry_Function_Copy_and_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 79 -->

### Website copy

THE GAPS CLIENTS REMEMBER
The Gaps Clients Remember
A beautiful website can lead to an indifferent appointment. A flawless event can be followed by no meaningful aftercare. Experience loses coherence at the handovers between marketing, retail, hospitality, operations and digital systems. We look at the moments where those divisions become visible to the client. The most useful change is not always a grand new environment; it may be the removal of a small indignity repeated thousands of times.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

PURPOSE
Describe a real sector or discipline problem before presenting Summit’s services. Keep this panel largely typographic; do not insert decorative icons.

## S13-P080 — What the Work Involves

### Website copy

WHAT THE WORK INVOLVES
What the Work Involves
Summit can map client and guest journeys, develop experience principles, design digital and physical touchpoints, plan immersive events and translate a brand’s positioning into service and communication behaviours. Where specialist expertise is required, collaborators are engaged to suit the brief, not presented as a permanent team by assumption. We work with the client to distinguish signature moments from operational basics that simply need to function.

## S13-P083 — Questions We Hear
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_13_Industry_Function_Copy_and_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 83 -->

### Website copy

FAQs
Questions We Hear
Do memorable experiences require large budgets?
Not always. Consistency, judgement and care can matter more than spectacle. The brief should identify which moments merit investment.
Does the digital experience belong in the same brief?
Usually, because clients do not divide their relationship with a maison into departments. The implementation can be staged where necessary.
How would we begin?
By understanding the brand promise, speaking to those who deliver it and examining the actual client journey before designing new moments.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

ACCORDION
Three manually edited questions. Closed by default, keyboard-operable and individually expandable. Keep questions indexable in the delivered HTML, not hidden behind client-side-only data.
No FAQ schema unless current search-engine eligibility and policy have been verified by the build team.
