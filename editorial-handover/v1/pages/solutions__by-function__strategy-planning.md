## S13-P058 — Clarity Before Activity
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_13_Industry_Function_Copy_and_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 58 -->

### Website copy

CLARITY BEFORE ACTIVITY
Clarity Before Activity
Strategy should not begin with a workshop full of adjectives. It begins with the actual proposition: what the organisation owns, makes, protects and can credibly promise. We consider brand architecture, category role, customer quality, market entry, growth discipline and the tension between short-term revenue and long-term standing. The objective is not to make a strategy document look comprehensive. It is to help a leadership team agree on decisions it can govern.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

PURPOSE
Describe a real sector or discipline problem before presenting Summit’s services. Keep this panel largely typographic; do not insert decorative icons.

## S13-P059 — What the Work Involves

### Website copy

WHAT THE WORK INVOLVES
What the Work Involves
An engagement may combine interviews, desk and market research, a current-position reading, opportunity testing and executive workshops. Outputs can include a sharpened positioning, audience and offer logic, decision principles or a staged action plan. The scope is set around the brief rather than a fixed template. Where luxury institutional questions are central, relevant Observatory findings may inform the judgement without substituting for primary evidence about the client.

## S13-P062 — Questions We Hear
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_13_Industry_Function_Copy_and_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 62 -->

### Website copy

FAQs
Questions We Hear
Can strategy help if the problem is still unclear?
Yes. Clarifying the decision is often the first useful piece of work. An initial conversation should identify the question before selecting a method.
Do we need a new brand identity?
Not necessarily. The work may reveal that the proposition or commercial discipline needs attention before visual expression.
What will an engagement produce?
That depends on the question agreed. We specify the decisions to resolve and the practical outputs before work begins.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

ACCORDION
Three manually edited questions. Closed by default, keyboard-operable and individually expandable. Keep questions indexable in the delivered HTML, not hidden behind client-side-only data.
No FAQ schema unless current search-engine eligibility and policy have been verified by the build team.
