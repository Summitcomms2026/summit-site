Progress Without Drift

## S13-P034 — Questions We Hear
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_13_Industry_Function_Copy_and_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 34 -->

### Website copy

FAQs
Questions We Hear
Is electrification itself a threat to luxury?
Not inherently. The test is whether the transition supports the maker’s recognisable character, product promise and ownership relationship.
Can a volume model support a luxury icon?
It can finance the institution that protects the icon. The concern begins when volume logic dictates the choices that define the house’s most important product.
Why examine the second-hand market?
Owner and resale behaviour can reveal whether the institution’s story still holds after the first transaction. It is a signal, not a prediction of price.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

ACCORDION
Three manually edited questions. Closed by default, keyboard-operable and individually expandable. Keep questions indexable in the delivered HTML, not hidden behind client-side-only data.
No FAQ schema unless current search-engine eligibility and policy have been verified by the build team.
