## S13-P037 — From Promise to Place
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_13_Industry_Function_Copy_and_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 37 -->

### Website copy

FROM PROMISE TO PLACE
From Promise to Place
A hotel, residence or private destination asks for trust before the guest has arrived. Architecture, service, booking, partnerships and after-stay contact must confirm that trust. The challenge is not merely to reach affluent audiences. It is to define which expectations the property can meet consistently and which associations will strengthen its character. A recognisable point of view is especially important when amenities, design references and marketing language become interchangeable.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

PURPOSE
Describe a real sector or discipline problem before presenting Summit’s services. Keep this panel largely typographic; do not insert decorative icons.

WHERE SUMMIT CAN HELP
Where Summit Can Help
We can work across naming, brand and market positioning, digital journeys, editorial storytelling, booking and enquiry experience, branded events, partnerships and private-client programmes. ## S13-P041 — Questions We Hear
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_13_Industry_Function_Copy_and_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 41 -->

### Website copy

FAQs
Questions We Hear
Can a property be premium without being luxury?
Yes. Excellent facilities or expensive finishes do not automatically produce a distinctive institutional or service proposition.
Where should a repositioning begin?
Often with the actual guest experience: who the property serves, what it can consistently deliver and which moments carry the greatest expectations.
Does digital booking conflict with personal service?
No. A good digital pathway should remove friction while preserving the possibility of appropriate human attention at the moments that matter.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

ACCORDION
Three manually edited questions. Closed by default, keyboard-operable and individually expandable. Keep questions indexable in the delivered HTML, not hidden behind client-side-only data.
No FAQ schema unless current search-engine eligibility and policy have been verified by the build team.
