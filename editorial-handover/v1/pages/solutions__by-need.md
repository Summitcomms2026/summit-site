## S02-P003 — Build a Maison
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_02_Solutions_By_Need_Build_a_Maison_v02.docx table 3 -->

### Website copy

BY NEED
Build a Maison
Give a promising luxury business the foundations of a house that can endure. Define what it stands for, what it makes, how it earns and what it must protect.
Reposition a Maison
Recover clarity without discarding what the house has earned. Reconsider its products, clients, market position and story before deciding what should change.
Enhance Desire Without Dilution
Build demand without surrendering the conditions that make the maison desirable. Examine distribution, pricing, communication and partnerships as decisions with consequences.
Strengthen an Icon
A successful object can carry a house or begin to confine it. Examine its meaning, its place in the range and the decisions that will preserve its authority.
Activate Patrimony & IP
Put archives, signatures and protected rights to work. Identify what can be renewed, what deserves protection and what must be left undisturbed.

## S02-P004 — Transform the Client Experience
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_02_Solutions_By_Need_Build_a_Maison_v02.docx table 4 -->

### Website copy

BY NEED
Transform the Client Experience
Bring service, physical spaces, digital encounters and private-client relationships into a coherent expression of the house.
Develop Thought Leadership
Establish a point of view worth hearing. Build it through research, editorial work and careful participation in the conversations that matter to the sector.
Applied AI
Use AI where it improves work, not as an emblem of modernity. Assess practical applications in research, service, communication and operations, with appropriate oversight.
Investment & Acquisitions
Consider the commercial opportunity alongside the institution being acquired, financed or developed. Clarify positioning, partner readiness and the assets that create lasting value.
Maison Due Diligence
Read beyond sales and brand recognition. Examine founding substance, iconic assets, governance, narrative dependence and exposures that may not yet appear in the accounts.
