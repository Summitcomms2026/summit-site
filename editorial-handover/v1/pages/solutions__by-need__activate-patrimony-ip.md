Make History Useful

### Website copy

WHAT WE EXAMINE
What Is Worth Keeping?
Start with the assets the house could not easily replace — including those it has never thought to catalogue.
Signatures & Rights
Names, forms, motifs, designs and their protection across products and markets.
Archives & Provenance
Drawings, records, objects and the evidence that gives an origin story its authority.
Craft & Know-how
Techniques, specialist relationships and skills at risk when people or suppliers move on.
Creative Use
What may return to the collection, enter an exhibition or inform a new commission — without turning history into costume.

## S04-P003 — An Archive Should Do More Than Sit There
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_04_Patrimony_Experience_Thought_Leadership_v01.docx table 3 -->

### Website copy

PATRIMONY
An Archive Should Do More Than Sit There
A display cabinet can preserve an object and still do very little for the house that made it. The stronger archive is useful: it informs design, proves authorship, supports restoration, clarifies rights and gives a younger generation something real to inherit.
That requires an accurate record, a policy for use and the judgement to know when leaving something alone is the better decision.

CROSS-SELL  Essay link serves as evidence, not as an assertion that Chanel or Charvet is a Summit client.

## S04-P004 — What Needs Protecting?
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_04_Patrimony_Experience_Thought_Leadership_v01.docx table 4 -->

### Website copy

EXPLORE THE QUESTIONS
What Needs Protecting?
Choose the part of the house you are concerned about. We’ll suggest the questions worth putting on the table.
A signature
Who owns the rights? How consistently is the form used? Where could its distinctiveness weaken?
The archive
What has been catalogued? What can still be verified? Which material is being used without a clear policy?
A craft or technique
Who holds the knowledge? How is it passed on? What happens if the next generation is not there?
An extension or collaboration
What is being lent to the partner? What returns to the house after the agreement ends?
Discuss Your Priority  →
Conversation guide only. Not a legal assessment.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

INTERACTION  Four-choice selector. Selecting one displays two to three specific questions and replaces the generic enquiry context.
FORMAT  Accessible buttons or two-column cards, one selected state. Immediate inline result. No progress bar or invented score.
CONVERSION  “Discuss Your Priority” opens editable /lets-talk route with selected concern and prompts, without automatically transmitting answers.
PRIVACY  No email gate. Responses remain in the browser until the visitor elects to send an enquiry.
PROTOTYPE  A small standalone working prototype accompanies this pass for the build session.
