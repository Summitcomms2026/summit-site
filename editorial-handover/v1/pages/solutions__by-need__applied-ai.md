## S05-P003 — Where Judgement Stays
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_05_Applied_AI_Investment_Maison_Due_Diligence_v01.docx table 3 -->

### Website copy

PUBLIC-FACING COPY
APPLIED AI
Where Judgement Stays
A maison can automate the retrieval of an archive record. It still needs someone who knows whether the record is true, whether it may be used and whether using it would be wise.
The same applies to client service, image-making, legal material and creative decisions. Accuracy, permissions, provenance and the house’s own standards are part of the work — not an afterthought once the system has been installed.
The question is not how much can be automated. It is what should be.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

CLIFF NOTES / PRODUCTION
RESEARCH  Consistent with Touch Me Not: the framework can read conditions behind meaning; technology does not manufacture aura.
IMAGE  Authentic analogue document beside a human verification process. Never suggest the machine itself authenticates an object.
LAYOUT  Single strong statement followed by compact prose. Consider a slim full-width photographic interruption.
OUTBOUND  Optional in-text link to Touch Me Not / The Mystery of Luxury, contingent on publication status.
