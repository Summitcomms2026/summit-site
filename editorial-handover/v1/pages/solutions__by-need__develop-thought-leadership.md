## S04-P014 — Start With an Argument
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_04_Patrimony_Experience_Thought_Leadership_v01.docx table 14 -->

### Website copy

EDITORIAL & INFLUENCE
Start With an Argument
A position needs more than a name beneath it. It needs a question worth answering, evidence that will survive scrutiny and a voice the intended audience can recognise.
Point of View
What does the person or institution know — or see differently — that warrants an argument?
Evidence
Which original findings, decisions or experiences make the case defensible?
Audience
Who needs to consider the argument, and what decision might it change?
The result may be a single essay or the foundation of a sustained editorial programme. Volume is not the test.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

LAYOUT  Three-column editorial structure. Keep column headings familiar and meaningful.
IMAGE  One understated pull-quote or real marginal note rather than icons.
INTERACTION  Optional three-question “Find Your Argument” guide; do not add here until the site has an actual submission pathway.
CONVERSION  A text link after the group may point to Editorial & Influence programme.

White papers, reports and evidence-led positions.
Public Voice
Speeches, presentations, conversations and executive narratives.
