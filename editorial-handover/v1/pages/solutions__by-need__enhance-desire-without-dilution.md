### Website copy

WHAT WE EXAMINE
Where Growth Changes the House
Distribution
Which channels increase access without giving away control of the client relationship.
Pricing
Where price supports confidence — and where promotions, gaps or inconsistency erode it.
Production and editions
Whether new lines and releases clarify the proposition or crowd its strongest objects.
Client quality
The difference between more customers and relationships worth keeping.
The objective is not less growth. It is revenue that leaves the maison stronger.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

PURPOSE  Use operational terms senior leaders already recognise. Avoid vague claims about aura being a metric.
IMAGE  None in the grid.
LAYOUT  Four compact categories in two columns; a single closing sentence beneath.
INTERACTION  Each item can link to the matching Demand Quality Check concern, but the grid must work without JavaScript.
CAUTION  Do not claim these are quantified Observatory scores or thresholds.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

PURPOSE  The primary interactive lead tool for this pass. Delivers value before asking for contact details.
FORMAT  Five single-select questions, short progress indicator, results drawn from selected concerns. No invented numerical score.
RESULT  Show “Your main consideration” plus “Before you decide”. Result changes with the reported constraint and next decision.
CTA  “Discuss This With Summit” carries a user-editable summary to Let’s Talk; “Copy My Summary” works without sending data.
CONSENT  Never automatically email results or add a respondent to a newsletter. Newsletter consent separate and unchecked.
FALLBACK  Non-JS page includes four pressure-point links and the general contact CTA.
HANDOVER  Exact questions, options, response mapping and acceptance tests appear in the build specification.
