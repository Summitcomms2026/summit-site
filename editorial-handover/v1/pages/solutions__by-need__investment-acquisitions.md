### Website copy

INVESTMENT & ACQUISITIONS
Look Beyond the Accounts
Turnover, margin and market share describe a business. They do not, on their own, explain why a maison may endure.
What It Owns
The rights, objects, archives, signatures and know-how that are genuinely controlled.
What It Depends On
Founders, craftspeople, suppliers, relationships and distribution arrangements that may not transfer cleanly.
What It Has Spent
Where overextension, licensing, discounting or borrowed narratives may have weakened desirability.
What Comes Next
The choices required to protect the proposition while developing its commercial future.

## S05-P010 — From Opportunity to Decision
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_05_Applied_AI_Investment_Maison_Due_Diligence_v01.docx table 10 -->

### Website copy

PUBLIC-FACING COPY
HOW WE WORK
From Opportunity to Decision
Our contribution is to make the luxury proposition clearer before a commitment is made. That may mean an institutional reading, a review of brand and revenue architecture or a programme to address weaknesses after a transaction.
Where the brief calls for legal, tax, regulated investment or valuation advice, the relevant professionals should lead those disciplines. Summit’s role is commercial and institutional, informed by original research.
A good acquisition still needs a good steward.
