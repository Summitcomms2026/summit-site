PUBLIC-FACING COPY
MAISON DUE DILIGENCE
What the Numbers Leave Out
A maison’s most valuable proposition may never appear as a separate line in a financial report. The warning signs may be just as difficult to spot.
The Object
Does the product have authority of its own, or is the house carrying it?
The Story
Who controls the record — the maison, the market or everybody else?
The Rights
Which names, forms, techniques and archives can the institution actually defend?
The Discipline
Are supply, distribution, collaborations and aftercare protecting desirability?
The Institution
Will the knowledge, governance and judgement survive a change of ownership?

## S05-P016 — Evidence Before Opinion

### Website copy

PUBLIC-FACING COPY
HOW WE WORK
Evidence Before Opinion
A useful reading begins with what can be established. We review the institutional proposition, examine the evidence available and make the unresolved questions visible rather than smoothing them away.
The Observatory informs the comparison. Appropriate legal, valuation, financial, tax and specialist disciplines remain independent requirements of any transaction.
The result is a clearer set of questions for the people making the decision — not a prediction of price or investment return.
Explore How The Observatory Works  →

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

CLIFF NOTES / PRODUCTION
RESEARCH  The Observatory distinguishes governed core entries, unscored outer field and unresolved evidence. Do not blur those categories.
LAYOUT  Two-column body; opposite a restrained, readable “What you receive” set of outputs (questions, evidence gaps, decision implications) marked as proposed engagement shape.
BOUNDARY  No promise to “certify” a maison, identify all risks, audit the report or supply financial forecasts.

Research for Better Questions
The Luxury 100 examines objects at the recognised centre of luxury. The Observatory studies the wider field and the institutional conditions behind it.
