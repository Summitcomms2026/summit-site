REPOSITION A MAISON
Change Course Without Losing the House
Familiarity can become a comfort long after it has stopped being an advantage. Markets move, catalogues expand and the story a maison tells may no longer match what clients see.
We help owners and leadership teams establish what still carries authority, what has drifted and what deserves to change — before commissioning a new identity.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

PURPOSE  Frame repositioning as a question of institutional judgement, not a cosmetic rebrand.
IMAGE  An original archive or product-development record alongside a contemporary detail. Evidence of continuity and change, not a generic “before and after”.
LAYOUT  Two-column opening, about 55% copy and 45% image. Headline lives outside the image.
ACTIONS  Primary opens Let’s Talk with “Reposition a Maison” selected. Secondary leads to How We Work; use a text link.
MOBILE  Stack copy, then the image. Keep both actions visible without a sticky pop-up.

## S03-P002 — What No Longer Fits?
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_03_Reposition_Desire_Icons_Copy_and_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 2 -->

### Website copy

WHAT WE EXAMINE
What No Longer Fits?
Repositioning often begins when one part of the house changes faster than the rest.
Clients
New buyers arrive, but the maison has not decided which relationships to deepen.
Product
Range growth makes the original proposition harder to recognise.
Position
Price, competitors and expectations move apart.
Story
Retailers, resellers or public commentary do more of the telling than the maison itself.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

PURPOSE  Give executives recognisable symptoms; none should imply that change is always required.
IMAGE  Type only. The distinctions need little help from photography.
LAYOUT  Four text-led cards in 2×2 columns at desktop. No four-card icon set.
ACTION  Each card may also select the corresponding choice in the interactive “What Needs Attention?” feature below.
MOBILE  Stack four short entries with dividers; no carousel.

## S03-P003 — Keep, Change, Leave Behind
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_03_Reposition_Desire_Icons_Copy_and_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 3 -->

### Website copy

HOW WE WORK
Keep, Change, Leave Behind
The work starts by distinguishing the foundations of the maison from the habits accumulated around them.
Keep
Products, skills, relationships and stories that still earn authority.
Change
The parts of the proposition, experience and commercial model no longer serving the house.
Leave behind
Extensions, claims and conventions the maison no longer needs to defend.
The result is a clear brief for action across brand, product, channels, client experience and communication — with the appropriate safeguards around patrimony and rights.
Discuss Repositioning  →

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

PURPOSE  Turn an abstract service into a decision framework without implying automatic rebranding.
IMAGE  No image. A three-part typographic composition should carry this section.
LAYOUT  Three columns, each with one short statement. Place the explanatory paragraph below, followed by a single mid-page CTA.
CTA  “Discuss Repositioning” opens the contact route with this page as source. No forced calendar.
CAUTION  Where legal or IP advice is required, specialist scope and engagement must be confirmed.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

PURPOSE  A fast, useful self-selection tool rather than a score or compulsory questionnaire.
LAYOUT  Horizontal four-choice selector. Selecting one shows a 40–60 word response and a tailored CTA; all choices remain visible.
LOGIC  Position → check price, peers, identity. Product → what carries the house. Clients → customer quality, occasions, rituals. Story → narrative ownership and archive.
CONVERSION  CTA opens Let’s Talk with selected topic in the enquiry. No personal data collected until the visitor chooses to submit a brief.
PRIVACY  No claim that this selector provides a diagnosis. Avoid saving answers without consent.
MOBILE  Four stacked option rows followed by one response. Keyboard focus and screen-reader labels required.
