## S03-P012 — Carrying or Carried?
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_03_Reposition_Desire_Icons_Copy_and_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 12 -->

### Website copy

STRENGTHEN AN ICON
Carrying or Carried?
An icon and its maison strengthen one another. The balance matters.
When the object carries the house
Its design, story and use keep drawing attention back to the institution that made it.
When the house carries the object
Recognition survives, but the object depends increasingly on advertising, distribution or borrowed prestige to sustain it.
Neither condition is a verdict by itself. The useful question is what decisions created the balance — and what might change it.
Discuss Your Icon  →

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

PURPOSE  Render the central original-research distinction in plain language; do not expose a false binary risk diagnosis.
LAYOUT  Two columns with matching short statements. A simple “Carrying / Carried” switch can toggle examples, but both statements remain accessible without scripting.
IMAGE  No new image: typography allows the distinction to work.
INTERACTION  Optional two-state explainer, not a scored quiz; click reveals what should be examined and a relevant enquiry CTA.

### Website copy

WHAT WE EXAMINE
Protect the Object. Make Room for the House
The object
Which details, materials and conventions must remain recognisable.
The editions
Whether extensions, collaborations or reissues add meaning or exhaust it.
The story
Who tells it now — the maison, its clients or the market around it.
The archive
What has been kept, understood and made useful rather than merely displayed.
The future
Where new creative work can emerge without asking the established icon to do everything.
