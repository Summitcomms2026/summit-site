## S11-P028 — May I quote or link to an essay?
<!-- source: Summit_Website_Pass_11_Lets_Talk_Utility_Footer_Copy_FAQs_Cliff_Notes_v01.docx table 28 -->

### Website copy

PUBLIC WEBSITE COPY   /   FAQ ACCORDIONS
May I quote or link to an essay?
Ordinary linking is welcome. Any request to reproduce substantial text, illustrations, figures or other protected material should identify the intended usage and be addressed through the relevant permissions process.
Can I rely on an Observatory reading to make an investment?
No. The Observatory provides an interpretive framework for institutional standing. Investment, financial, legal and valuation decisions require the appropriate professional advice and independently verified evidence.
Can the terms be updated?
The approved Terms should state how updates take effect. Material changes should be presented with an identifiable effective date so visitors can understand which version applied.

### Photography, layout and interaction notes

CLIFF NOTES   /   FAQ BEHAVIOUR
LAYOUT  Native, accessible accordion rows; answers remain indexable and readable without submitting a form.
INTERACTION  Keyboard support, clear focus, correct expanded state and restrained chevron. Do not use a button merely to decorate.
MOBILE  Full-width tap targets, short question lines and comfortable text measure.
EDIT  Do not use FAQs to make policy or contractual claims before operations and legal approval.
CONVERSION  A quiet contextual CTA may follow the complete FAQ section; never interrupt an expanded answer.
