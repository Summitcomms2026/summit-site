Principles

Six working principles.

01

Start with the question

Summit enters through the problem, not through a discipline it has already decided to sell. The first piece of work is often to state the question precisely enough to answer it.

02

Senior attention

The people who frame the question remain involved in the work. Judgement is not handed down a chain.

03

Research when it matters

Research is used when it changes the quality of a decision — not as theatre around a recommendation already made.

04

Disciplines follow the problem

Strategy, product, patrimony, experience, editorial, digital and technology combine where the question requires them, and not where it does not.

05

Specialists enter deliberately

Collaborators join where specialised expertise materially improves the work, with their role and responsibility made clear.

06

The output must be useful

The objective is not another strategic artefact. It is a decision, an architecture, a programme, an experience or a body of work that can be used.
