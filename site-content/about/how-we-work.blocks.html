FAQs

What should we bring to the first conversation?

A clear account of what you are trying to decide is enough. Context helps, but a fully written brief is not required. Avoid sharing confidential contracts, client lists or unreleased financial material through an ordinary enquiry form.

Do we have to commission a diagnostic first?

No. Sometimes a short defined assessment is appropriate; sometimes the next step is already evident. We recommend a scope because it serves the decision, not because every relationship must begin with the same product.

What will the engagement deliver?

Outputs depend on the agreed brief. They may include a strategic assessment, a position, a programme of work, a digital experience, communications or a specialist reading. Deliverables, decision points and responsibilities should be agreed before work begins.

Can we discuss sensitive matters confidentially?

Yes, subject to suitable arrangements. Begin by outlining the nature of the question without disclosing sensitive details. The appropriate confidentiality, security and professional terms can then be discussed before private documents are exchanged.

Does private work influence the published Canon?

No. The public Canon and Observatory research should remain editorially governed and independent of paid client engagements. Private advisory use can draw on relevant methods without changing public rankings or turning research into a purchased endorsement.
