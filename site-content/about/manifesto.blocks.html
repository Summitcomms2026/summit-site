FAQs

Does restraint mean avoiding growth?

No. The question is whether growth strengthens the institution or spends the very qualities that make it desirable. Wider distribution, new categories and new audiences can be valuable; the terms matter.

Will you challenge a client’s brief?

When the evidence calls for it. Respect includes explaining concerns clearly, not simply agreeing. A useful disagreement should improve the decision, not become a performance of independence.

Is your thinking anti-technology?

No. Technology is useful where it removes friction, reveals evidence or improves an experience. It becomes less useful when a tool is allowed to make cultural, creative or institutional judgements for which people remain responsible.

How do you distinguish evidence from opinion?

The distinction should be visible in the work. We identify source material, explain the assumptions behind a recommendation and make clear where professional judgement enters. Not every question produces a number, and a number is not automatically an answer.
