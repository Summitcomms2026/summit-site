These are not values Summit claims for itself. They are conditions the work keeps finding in the houses and objects that endure — and keeps finding missing in those that do not.

01 / 06

Stewardship

Heritage is not stewardship.

Age proves only that something has lasted. Stewardship is the work of keeping its meaning intact: protected rather than merely monetised, renewed without being spent.

02 / 06

Restraint

What a house refuses can carry more authority than what it extends.

Restraint leaves marks as legible as action — a history allowed to close, a silence kept, a scarcity chosen again each year. Each has a cost, and each can be lost.

03 / 06

Authority

Fame is not authority.

Fame is recognition. Authority is the influence that remains when attention has moved elsewhere, earned slowly by the same object meaning the same thing for a long time.

04 / 06

Memory

A maison without memory eventually has only marketing.

Archives, techniques and the people who hold them in hand are not decoration. Kept as a working library, they let a house wake what has been sleeping. Neglected, they leave it nothing of its own to say.

05 / 06

Governance

Governance is not a moral category. It is a durability condition.

What a house protects, repeats, refuses, licenses, archives and relinquishes becomes part of what it is. The decisions are rarely visible. Their consequences always are.

06 / 06

Judgement

Data can describe conditions. It cannot decide what matters.

Instruments are worth building, and worth publishing. The best of them does not remove judgement. It compels judgement to declare itself.

Quality of judgement makes luxury endure.
