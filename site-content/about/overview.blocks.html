FAQs

Is Summit a creative agency or an adviser?

Both disciplines belong in the practice. Summit works on strategic questions and can develop the communications, experiences or other creative work they require. The appropriate scope depends on the decision rather than a fixed menu of production services.

Is the Observatory a separate company?

No. The Observatory is the research instrument within Summit’s wider practice. Its public research and editorial judgements remain independent of paid private engagements. The client engages Summit, not the instrument.

Can we speak before we know what work is needed?

Yes. You should not need to arrive with a perfect brief. We will ask what is at stake, what you already know and which decision is proving difficult. The first conversation is a chance to establish whether further work would be useful.
