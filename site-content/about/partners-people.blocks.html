Founder, Summit Communication Group

Gregory Gray

Gregory came to luxury through strategy rooms, entertainment engagements and luxury communication — and through a growing impatience with the ways the industry describes itself: by taste, by finance or by heritage theatre, none of which distinguishes price from meaning, or age from stewardship.

He founded Summit Communication Group and writes The Future of Luxury. With Olivia Dhordain he is co-author of Touch Me Not: The Mystery of Luxury, for which he built the instrument behind The Luxury 100 and The Observatory.

Olivia Dhordain

Olivia is an intellectual-property lawyer whose career has been spent inside the protection of luxury: seventeen years in-house for some of the most aspirational maisons in the world, much of it defending products, motifs and house signatures that had moved beyond the tidy protection of a registered right.

She practises from Geneva through her own firm, Outboxing-IP, on luxury IP, patrimony and institutional governance. She is co-author of Touch Me Not, and the ink-and-gold drawings at the thresholds of its chapters are hers.

Where an engagement turns on what a house can own, protect and pass on, Summit and Outboxing-IP may work together. Legal advice is given by Outboxing-IP under its own engagement.

Specialist collaboration

A senior core. Specialists where they change the result.

Beyond its core, Summit assembles specialists around each engagement — strategists, designers, writers, producers, developers and legal counsel. They join where their expertise materially improves the work, and the people who framed the question remain responsible for it.
