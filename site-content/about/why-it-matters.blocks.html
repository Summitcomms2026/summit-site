The pressure

Scale has changed what luxury is asked to do.

Houses are expected to grow, to be visible everywhere and to produce for every channel at once. None of that is wrong in itself. Unexamined, each of it pulls a house towards the ordinary.

What is being mistaken

Eight substitutions.

Attention for authority

Reach is measured daily. Authority shows only over decades, and it is easily spent to buy the first.

Visibility for standing

Being seen everywhere is not the same as mattering somewhere.

Heritage performed for heritage kept

An archive used as decoration is not an archive at work.

Extension for growth

Product extension without institutional logic adds revenue and subtracts meaning.

Personalisation for attention

Generic personalisation treats a client as a segment. Luxury depends on treating one as a person.

Speed for decisiveness

The pressure to move quickly is real. So is the cost of deciding before the question is understood.

Imitation for relevance

Following the category is the fastest way to resemble it.

Storage for memory

Archives that no one governs become vulnerable — to loss, to misuse, and to others who will tell the story instead.

Abundance

When acceptable output is cheap, deciding becomes the work.

Intelligent systems are lowering the cost of producing acceptable content, images, interfaces and recommendations. That is useful, and Summit uses such systems where they help. It also moves the value elsewhere — to deciding:

  • what should exist, and what should not;
  • what belongs to the house;
  • what must remain human;
  • what deserves to be repeated.

These are not technical questions. They are questions of judgement and stewardship, and they become more valuable as everything around them becomes easier to make.

The constructive case

The rarer capability is no longer production, distribution or attention. It is judgement.
