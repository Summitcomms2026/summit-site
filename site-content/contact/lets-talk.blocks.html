FAQs

Do I need to know which service to ask for?

No. Tell us the decision, opportunity or difficulty you are considering. If another part of the practice is more relevant, we can discuss that after understanding your situation.

Is the first conversation a sales presentation?

It should not be. We begin by listening and testing our understanding. We may ask for context or explain the kinds of work that could be useful, but the conversation is not a commitment to appoint Summit.

What information should I send?

Your name, a way to reach you and a few sentences about the subject are enough. You do not need to send a formal brief, budget or presentation. We would rather arrange a suitable process before sensitive material is shared.

Can we speak confidentially?

We treat serious enquiries with discretion. A public contact form, however, is not a secure channel for privileged or highly sensitive information. If confidentiality arrangements are required, please flag that without sending the material itself.

What happens if Summit is not the right adviser?

We should say so. The first conversation is intended to clarify the question and determine whether there is a useful role for us. An appointment should follow from a real fit, not a polished pitch.

Can we arrange a conversation across time zones?

Yes, an international enquiry can specify its location and a convenient time window. Scheduling details are arranged after the initial contact; we should not imply that a fixed appointment has been booked until it is confirmed.
