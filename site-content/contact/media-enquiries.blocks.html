FAQs

Can Summit comment on a particular luxury maison?

Our published research addresses institutions and their behaviour across the luxury sector. Whether we can comment on a particular situation depends on the evidence available, the scope of the question and any relevant professional commitments.

Can I reproduce a chart, image or book illustration?

Please request permission for the specific item and intended use. Material appearing on the website may be subject to rights held by authors, collaborators, photographers or third parties.

Can we interview Gregory Gray or Olivia Dhordain?

Requests for Gregory Gray may be submitted here. Olivia Dhordain is an independent co-author and specialist collaborator; any request concerning her involvement should be considered and agreed rather than assumed.

How quickly will you reply?

Please include your deadline and time zone. We will assess the request, but cannot guarantee availability or a response within a particular period.
