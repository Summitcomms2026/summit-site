02 — Field and canon

The Field is not a second canon.

The Luxury 100 settles which objects constitute the canonical centre. The Field maps the propositions around that centre under the same intellectual discipline, and asks a different question of them.

The Canon Ranks. A closed set of one hundred objects at the constitutional centre, each traceable to declared evidence.

The Field Classifies. Propositions from the same territory, in different positions within it — most of them not weaker than the Canon, but differently placed.

The Field is not a waiting room. Some of its propositions may be read for constitutional admission in later editions; most will not, because constitutional significance and institutional seriousness are different categories.

03 — Arguable and Remote

Two readings, both relative to a sector.

Each scored entry is read against the canonical standard of its own sector and receives one of two classifications.

Arguable Within reasonable argument of the canonical level for its sector.

Remote Not within reasonable argument of that level.

  • Not grades of quality
  • Not investment categories
  • Not universal ranks
  • Not a queue for the Canon

The canonical bar in Watches sits at a different point from the bar in Fragrance. An entry that reads Arguable in one sector could read Remote in another.

05 — Open edges

A governed instrument records where its map ends.

The Field does not smooth over what it cannot yet read. Its open edges are declared, each for a stated reason.

Evidence awaited

Where primary-source confirmation has been sought from the institution that holds it, the entry waits rather than scoring on inferred dates or assumed forms.

Methodological limits

Some sectors press against the instrument itself: consumable propositions whose formulas change over time, and travel propositions that resist a single bounded form. These limits are declared, not adjusted away.

Propositions held

Where a bounded form is disputed or an institution is in transition, the proposition is held rather than rushed into a reading the evidence cannot yet support.

The outer horizon

Beyond the scored field lies wider territory the Observatory records but does not score. It disciplines future reading; it is not a second list.

These are not weaknesses to apologise for. They are where the honesty of the instrument becomes visible.
