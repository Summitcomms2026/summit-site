Questions Worth Asking

Can you examine a maison that is not in The Luxury 100?

Yes, subject to a defined question and sufficient evidence. The Canon is not a list of eligible clients. A private reading can examine a maison’s products, archive and institutional conditions, while recognising that not every proposition satisfies the Canon’s bounded-object rules.

Can this tell us whether to extend or discontinue a product?

It can clarify what the decision may put at risk: identity coherence, scarcity, story ownership or an overdependence on one object. It cannot decide a product strategy in isolation. Commercial data, manufacturing realities and the judgement of the maison’s leadership still matter.

What if the problem is growth rather than heritage?

The two need not be separate. Distribution, entry tiers, client experience and collaboration can grow revenue while altering the institution’s future standing. We examine those choices together, distinguishing productive expansion from growth that spends the very reason clients desired the house.

Will we be given a rank or an aura score?

Not as a substitute for diagnosis. A useful private brief begins with the institutional question and the evidence, not a headline number. Any analytical outputs, limits and confidence would need to be agreed within the scope of the work.
