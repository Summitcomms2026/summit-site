What the Method Looks At

Definition

A recognisable, bounded proposition with a verifiable history, where the object-based rules apply.

Evidence

Origination, supply discipline, continuity, mythology, governance and the material required to read them.

Interpretation

A structured reading of patterns, supported by qualitative notes and explicit distinctions between evidence and inference.

Confidence

What is secure, what remains provisional and where a primary source would change the reading.

Questions Worth Asking

Can the method measure aura?

It does not treat aura as a property that a researcher can simply assign by instinct. The published Canon derives an aura reading from documented patterns across other dimensions, subject to qualitative constraints. That reading is an interpretation of evidence, not a machine that creates mystery.

Is this done by artificial intelligence?

The work emerged from a question about what data and AI can reveal, but no machine is entitled to make the judgement on its own. Evidence selection, boundary decisions, interpretation and confidence remain matters of accountable research. We would not present an automated result as a considered institutional opinion.

Can we question the findings?

Yes. A method worth using must tolerate disagreement. The research sets out definitions, evidence boundaries and confidence so that a reading can be challenged on its terms. Where a primary source changes the evidence, the research should acknowledge that rather than defend a convenient answer.

Has every finding been independently audited?

No blanket claim of external audit should be made. The Luxury 100 2027 edition expressly states that no external body audited that edition. The work’s credibility rests on its declared evidence, method and limits; any future external review should be described only when it has actually occurred.
