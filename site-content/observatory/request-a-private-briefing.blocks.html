01 — Scope

What a briefing may consider.

  • Maison or portfolio position
  • Sector context
  • Authority and fame
  • Governance
  • Patrimony and institutional memory
  • Dependence on icons
  • Canonical proximity
  • Questions of stewardship

A briefing is a structured reading, shaped by the question in front of it. It is not investment, valuation or legal advice.

02 — Separation

Public instrument. Private interpretation.

Private application interprets a specific context. It cannot purchase inclusion, move a published reading or revise a public finding — that separation is what allows both to be taken seriously.
