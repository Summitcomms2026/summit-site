What the Numbers Miss

Average order value, acquisition cost and conversion matter. So do the consequences they do not reveal: demand drawn by discounting, loss of client ownership, overdependence on platforms or a shopping experience that makes a prized object feel ordinary.

The objective is not to reject measurement. It is to measure what strengthens the business.

Good Questions

Is this a performance marketing programme?

It can include assessment of acquisition channels and campaign performance, but the remit is wider. It considers the quality of demand, the coherence of the digital experience, client relationships and the commercial decisions that may weaken long-term desirability.

Can you give us conversion or revenue targets?

Useful targets follow a review of existing systems, data quality and the commercial constraints the maison intends to keep. A promise made before that work would be a guess rather than a plan.

Will we need to rebuild our website?

Not necessarily. The priority is to identify where the current journey loses clarity, confidence or commercial opportunity. Content, navigation, assistance or measurement may need attention before a platform change is justified.

How does this connect with Brand & IP Stewardship?

Commerce creates new exposures: distribution control, product imagery, client data, resale narratives and the use of protected marks. Growth decisions should be considered alongside the rights and institutional identity they affect, with specialist advice where legal questions arise.
