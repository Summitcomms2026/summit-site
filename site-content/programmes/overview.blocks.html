I What makes a Programme

Defined does not mean standardised.

A Programme establishes a governed starting architecture. The work itself remains specific to the maison, its audience and its question.

Programmes are not packages or tiers, and they do not replace bespoke strategic work. They give shape to engagements Summit is asked for often enough to deserve one.
