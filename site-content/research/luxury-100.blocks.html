What it reads

Not the hundred best things.

The Canon is a fixed set of one hundred positions in luxury culture: not the hundred best things, the most expensive or the ones to buy, but the places a serious account of the subject has to occupy — and the objects currently occupying them.

They are durable objects in which material craft became symbolic meaning, held by institutions that can protect that meaning or spend it. Every position is read again each year against the same framework, and given a verdict for the year.

The 2027 reading

Stillness makes change legible.

  • 80 of 99 recorded no material event
  • 86 held
  • 9 tended
  • 2 strained
  • 2 mending

The year was almost uneventful, and that is the finding. When nearly everything holds still, whatever stirs shows clearly — and it was almost never the market. In nearly every case it was a decision taken inside the house that owns the object.

Verdicts

How each year is read.

  • Held The object’s standing is unchanged this year.
  • Tended Something was done to it, or around it, that required care.
  • Strained Its standing is under pressure, usually from its own ubiquity.
  • Mending Damage is being repaired, and the repair is visible.
  • Sealed Manufacture is over. Judgement now concerns custody.
  • Quiet year A quiet year, verified: no material annual event.

The empty chair

One chair is empty.

[summit_research_chair empty="83"]

For years the place was held by a French silver service, ranked for its modernism and for the tables of the Republic. Read properly, the modernism belonged to another designer’s work entirely. The right silver was found and scored against the same framework as everything else; neither candidate reached the line.

The line could have been lowered. The chair is printed empty instead.

A canon that cannot leave a chair empty is not a canon. It is a catalogue.

Stewardship

The same faculty, pointed the other way.

The houses that hold these objects keep the archives, employ the last people who can do particular things with their hands, and decline the obvious commercial move. The same houses can extend a name until it means very little. Stewardship is not the opposite of that risk; it is the same capacity, pointed the other way.

A still year shows which way it is currently pointing. The Observatory reads the same pattern across the wider field.
