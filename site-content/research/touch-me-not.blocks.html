The Object

Price and fame can tell us what a market recognises. They cannot, by themselves, explain why meaning endures.

The Maison

An object can carry a house. Sometimes the house is carrying the object, long after its own authority has weakened.

The Decision

The most consequential acts are frequently those not taken: licences refused, editions withheld, production constrained, archives kept alive.

The Limit

A disciplined instrument can examine the conditions of aura. It cannot manufacture aura or guarantee future value.

Good Questions

Is this a book about luxury brands or luxury objects?

Objects are the starting point. Their manufacture, histories, meanings and governance reveal something about the institutions behind them. The book is not a conventional ranking of companies.

Does it claim to measure aura?

No. The research tests the conditions associated with aura through evidence and a disclosed method. Its own argument recognises what cannot be reduced to a number.

Can the findings be used in a board discussion?

They can sharpen questions about distribution, licensing, patrimony, iconic products and continuity. They do not replace the board’s own facts, legal advice or commercial judgement.

Can we discuss how the work relates to our maison?

Yes. A conversation can establish the decision you are facing and whether a more structured private reading would be useful. Public research positions are never changed by a commercial engagement.
