The New Asian Maison

How Cultural Specificity Becomes Global Luxury

  • From Summit Communication Group
  • Year 2026
  • Built from The Summit Observatory

Asia’s role in global luxury is shifting from consumption to authorship. The paper argues that the decade’s most consequential story will be how many globally significant maisons Asia builds, and whether they are built to endure — reading Asia as a system of markets rather than a single bloc.

It discloses no scoring formula, pillar weights or numerical scores, and it is not investment, legal or tax advice.
