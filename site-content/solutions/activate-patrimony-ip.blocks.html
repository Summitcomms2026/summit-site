PATRIMONY

An Archive Should Do More Than Sit There

A display cabinet can preserve an object and still do very little for the house that made it. The stronger archive is useful: it informs design, proves authorship, supports restoration, clarifies rights and gives a younger generation something real to inherit.

That requires an accurate record, a policy for use and the judgement to know when leaving something alone is the better decision.
