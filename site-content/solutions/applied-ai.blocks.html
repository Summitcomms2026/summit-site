APPLIED AI

Where Judgement Stays

A maison can automate the retrieval of an archive record. It still needs someone who knows whether the record is true, whether it may be used and whether using it would be wise.

The same applies to client service, image-making, legal material and creative decisions. Accuracy, permissions, provenance and the house’s own standards are part of the work — not an afterthought once the system has been installed.

The question is not how much can be automated. It is what should be.
