MAISON FORMATION

What Matters from the Start

Six foundations considered together. Not to make a young house look older, but to help it make decisions it can stand by.

Founding proposition

A cultural and creative position the house can credibly own.

Product

A recognisable object or product proposition capable of carrying that position.

Commercial model

The right clients, price structure, channels and quality of revenue.

Client experience

Service, private-client relationships and every physical or digital encounter.

IP and patrimony

Rights, records, signatures and knowledge that require protection.

Governance

How the house decides, what it refuses and what must survive a change of leadership.

HOW WE WORK

How to Begin

Maison Readiness Map

A focussed assessment of the founding proposition, product, commercial model, client experience, rights and governance. The outcome is a Maison Formation Diagnostic: principal risks, opportunities and a practical sequence of decisions for the next 90 days, 180 days and 12 months.

Revenue to Rights Sprint

A shorter commercial engagement for a house that needs its revenue to support the foundations of its future. We examine high-value clients, conversion, channels and editorial priorities, then connect near-term growth to the rights and patrimony that require attention.

Where Research Helps

The Observatory examines what gives luxury objects lasting authority and what puts that authority at risk. Its findings help us consider a young maison’s founding proposition, commercial discipline, control of its story and willingness to refuse the wrong opportunities.

Research sharpens judgement. It cannot grant a young house a history or manufacture desire.
