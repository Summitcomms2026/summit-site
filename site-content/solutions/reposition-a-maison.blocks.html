HOW WE WORK

Keep, Change, Leave Behind

The work starts by distinguishing the foundations of the maison from the habits accumulated around them.

Keep

Products, skills, relationships and stories that still earn authority.

Change

The parts of the proposition, experience and commercial model no longer serving the house.

Leave behind

Extensions, claims and conventions the maison no longer needs to defend.

The result is a clear brief for action across brand, product, channels, client experience and communication — with the appropriate safeguards around patrimony and rights.
