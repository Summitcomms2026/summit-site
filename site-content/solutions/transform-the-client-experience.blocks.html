HOW WE WORK

Design the Relationship, Not Just the Room

Work begins with the people, the objects and the promises the maison already makes. We review the existing experience, agree what should remain unmistakably its own and develop the changes that matter most.

That may involve client journeys, service language, digital touchpoints, appointment rituals, private events, aftercare or the systems supporting them.

When a brief calls for specialist experience and innovation work, we assemble the appropriate collaborators. The client engages Summit; the practice is shaped around the brief.
