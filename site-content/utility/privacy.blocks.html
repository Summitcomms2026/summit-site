What this site receives

This site collects personal information only when you send it:

  • through Let’s Talk, Media Enquiries or a Private Briefing request — your name, email address, organisation where given, and your message;
  • through a subscribe form — your email address, and any optional details the form asks for.

Submissions are kept as private records on Summit’s website and sent to Summit by email. They are used to reply to you, or to send The Future of Luxury to subscribers.

Questions about your information, or requests to have it removed, can be sent through Let’s Talk.
