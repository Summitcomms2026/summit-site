Enquiries and Subscriptions Are Different

Contact details supplied with an enquiry are for responding to that enquiry. Receiving The Future of Luxury should be a separate choice you can make or decline.

FAQs

Will a contact enquiry add me to the newsletter?

It should not. A newsletter subscription is a separate, optional action and should be recorded and managed independently of the reason you contacted Summit.

What this site receives

This site collects personal information only when you send it:

  • through Let’s Talk, Media Enquiries or a Private Briefing request — your name, email address and message, and anything optional you choose to add: organisation, telephone, topic, the type and deadline of a media request, or the kind of briefing;
  • through a subscribe form, or the separate box on Let’s Talk — your email address, and any optional details the form asks for.

Submissions are kept as private records on Summit’s website and sent to Summit by email. They are used to reply to you, or to send The Future of Luxury to subscribers. Ticking the separate box on Let’s Talk records a subscription of its own; an enquiry alone does not.

Questions about your information, or requests to have it removed, can be sent through Let’s Talk.
